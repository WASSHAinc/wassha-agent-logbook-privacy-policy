Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Wassha Agent Logbook (the "App") is developed and operated by Wassha Inc. ("we", "us", "our"). The App is used by Wassha's authorized agents to register customers, record equipment rentals, and view passcodes. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when the App is used, including information about the customers that agents register. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Agent account information: agent ID, name, and the password used to log in to the App.
  • Customer information entered by agents: when an agent registers or updates a customer, the App collects the customer's full name, phone number, government ID number, a photo of the government ID, a photo of the customer, village, address, email address (optional), next of kin name and phone number (optional), customer type (home or business), and the NFC tag ID or QR code ID linked to the customer.
  • Rental and logbook data: equipment rentals and returns, product and stock details, passcode history, timestamps, and related records of daily agent operations.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version, and app instance identifiers.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: screens viewed, feature usage, crash reports, and performance data, collected automatically through Google Firebase to help us maintain and improve the App.

The App does not collect location data, contacts, or financial account details.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's core functionality (customer registration, rental records, and passcode history).
  • To identify customers, verify their identity, and link them to the equipment they rent.
  • To verify agent identity and activity for business reporting purposes.
  • To monitor, analyze, and improve the performance and reliability of the App.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or misuse.
  • To communicate with users about updates, support, or operational matters.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • With Wassha group companies and affiliates for business operations and reporting.
  • With service providers who perform services on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations: Amazon Web Services (cloud hosting and storage of App data and photos) and Google Firebase (Analytics for usage data, Crashlytics for crash reports, and Remote Config for app settings). Google's use of this data is described in the Firebase privacy information.
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Wassha, our users, or others.

4. Data Storage and Security

Information collected through the App is sent to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on secure cloud servers. Customer records and photos are also kept on the agent's device until they are synced, so that the App can work without a network connection. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal and activity data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including for record-keeping, business reporting, and legal compliance. When data is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymized.

6. Permissions Used by the App

The App may request the following device permissions, which are used solely to provide its core features:

  • Camera: to take customer and government ID photos, and to scan customer QR codes.
  • NFC: to read the NFC tag linked to a customer.
  • Network access: to sync customer, rental, and passcode data with our servers.
  • Foreground service (data sync): to finish syncing data in the background.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by authorized agents and business personnel and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to certain processing. These rights apply to agents and to the customers whose information agents enter in the App. Customers can make a request through their Wassha agent or by contacting us directly using the details below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Effective date".
